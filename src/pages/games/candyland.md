Hey gamers!!! This is our first Game,

Its name is CandyLand.

This game is made for Beginners Circle Game Jam #2.

### Controls:
Arrow keys to move, Space Bar to jump, Left Shift to sprint.

### Story:
In this game, you are injured and ill .You have to go to hospital .But the twist is that there is a lava river . You have to cross the lava river to go to the hospital. There is a candy bridge, too. So, cross the lava river by going through the candy bridge. The lava is orange and ground in green

But, If you touch the lava river then you lose and if you pass the 3 levels then you win this game.

Good luck :D, Feedback is appreciated.

You can play it at [our itch.io site](https://asstudios.itch.io/candyland)
